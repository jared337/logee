The AI transport management system built for road freight

Road freight looks simple until it isn't: a truck either shows up or it doesn't, a rate confirmation either matches the invoice or it doesn't, and a proof of delivery either gets signed and filed or it quietly goes missing until someone asks for it. For SMB importers and exporters running a last-mile leg or a full cross-border haul, that simplicity hides the same visibility gap as ocean and air freight, just compressed into a shorter, faster-moving window.

logee is an AI-powered transport management system (TMS) that runs alongside road freight the same way it does every other mode. It reads the rate confirmation and bill of lading you already have, builds a live shipment record, and watches pickup, transit and delivery so a missed appointment or a mismatched invoice doesn't sit unnoticed until month-end.

Because logee understands what a truckload or LTL shipment actually is, the bill of lading, pickup and delivery appointment windows, accessorial charges like detention and liftgate, and the proof of delivery that closes it out, it does the coordination work a dispatcher would: confirm the appointment, watch the transit, check the invoice line by line, and flag a missing POD before it becomes a dispute. Road freight questions, answered

What is an AI TMS for road freight?
An AI transport management system (TMS) is software that organises and manages your shipments and uses AI to do the coordination work. logee reads your rate confirmations and bills of lading, builds a live record of every road shipment, tracks pickup through delivery, and flags a missed appointment or an invoice above quote.
Does logee handle both FTL and LTL?
Yes. logee tracks both full truckload (FTL) and less than truckload (LTL) shipments, and the milestones, appointment windows and accessorial charges that apply to each.
How does logee track detention and accessorial charges?
logee reads the rate confirmation's accessorial terms, detention, liftgate, inside delivery, and checks the final invoice against them, so an unexpected accessorial charge is flagged rather than absorbed.
Do I have to change my carrier or broker?
No. logee works with the carriers and brokers you already use. There's nothing for them to install, and they keep communicating with you by email exactly as they do today.
How does logee handle proof of delivery (POD)?
logee tracks whether a POD has been filed against the shipment and flags one that's missing after delivery, so it's chased while it's still easy to get rather than during a billing dispute weeks later.
How is this different from a carrier's own portal?
A carrier portal shows you that carrier's loads in their format. logee brings every shipment, across every carrier and broker you use, into one place, ties it to your costs and documents, and alerts you rather than waiting for you to check.
