Visibility & tracking

Know where everything is, without asking

One live picture of every shipment, grounded in carrier data rather than status-request emails.

One live shipment view

Every order in a single list with status, current stage, ETA and quoted-versus-actual cost. Filter by on time, watch, late or delivered.

The 8-stage timeline

One shared model for every shipment, from origin and type to proof of delivery, recording each ETA revision so you see every slip.

Carrier milestone feed

Raw carrier events, gate-in, loaded, departed, arrival, sit alongside the business timeline, so status is grounded in the carrier's own data.

Arriving this week

Multi-PO shipments

Quotes & booking

Procure freight in a few clicks

Turn a scattered RFQ process into one clear decision, with the agent doing the legwork.

Multi-forwarder RFQ

Send one request to the forwarders you already use and collect their quotes against the order, instead of chasing replies across email.

Side-by-side comparison

Price, transit time and terms lined up like for like, so the trade-offs are obvious at a glance rather than buried in threads.

Agent recommendation

Plain-language guidance on price versus reliability, weighing transit time, transshipment and on-time rate by lane. The agent recommends; you decide.

One-click booking

Book your choice and logee sets the forwarder and price, advances the shipment and declines the other quotes automatically.

Book direct or via forwarders

Request quotes from several forwarders, or book direct with a carrier when that is the simpler path.

Cargo-ready aware

Bookings line up with your cargo-ready date, so you do not pay to sit or miss a sailing you were counting on.

Landed cost as it lands

Month-to-date spend, what is awaiting payment and what is not yet invoiced, so cash and cost are never a surprise.

Approve or query

Approve and schedule payment, or open an agent-drafted query to the forwarder, right from the shipment.

Line-item breakdown

Ocean freight, fuel, terminal handling, storage and documentation, each compared to quote so you can trace the exact source of a variance.
