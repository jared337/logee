Why freight feels so hard

Six problems you already know too well

It isn't you. When shipments live across inboxes, spreadsheets and phone calls, things slip, and it costs you.

“My shipment info is buried across dozens of email threads. I only find out what something actually cost at the end of the month.”

Scattered across email

Quotes, bookings, documents and invoices sit in different threads with no shared view.

No single source of truth

Status, paperwork and pricing live in separate places. Nothing ties them to one shipment.

Costs surface too late

Deadlines get missed

Free time runs out and cut-offs pass, and the first sign is a demurrage or storage bill.

Chasing wastes time

Following up forwarders, brokers and suppliers by hand. Silence until you ask, then 'see attached'.

It lives in one person's head

When the person who knows the shipments is away, nobody else can find anything.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything importers and exporters usually ask before they start.

What exactly is logee?
logee is an AI transport management system (TMS) for importers and exporters. It reads your inbox and connected systems, builds a live record of every shipment, tracks it end to end, checks invoices against quotes, and flags problems like demurrage before they cost you.
How does logee get my shipment data?
You connect the email you already use, Gmail or Microsoft 365, and your ERP if you have one. logee reads the booking confirmations, documents and invoices that already land there and turns them into structured shipment records automatically. No manual data entry.
Do my forwarders, brokers or suppliers need to sign up?
No. They keep working over email exactly as they do today. logee reads and drafts the emails for you, so nobody on the other side needs a login or a new tool. You can optionally give a forwarder a limited view of just their own shipments.
Which inboxes, carriers and systems do you work with?
Gmail and Microsoft 365 for email, major ocean carriers such as Maersk, MSC, ZIM and Hapag-Lloyd for tracking, and ERPs like Priority. We're adding integrations throughout early access, so tell us what you use and we'll prioritise it.
Will logee ever email or book anything without my approval?
Never. logee drafts chasers and flags decisions, but nothing is sent and nothing is booked until you review and approve it. You stay in control of every outbound action.
How long does it take to get set up?
A couple of minutes. Connect your inbox and logee starts building your shipments straight away. There's nothing to install and nothing for your team to learn.
Is it really free?
Yes. logee has a free plan, with no credit card and no sales call. Paid plans (Starter, TMS Light and Enterprise) cover higher volumes and advanced features, and you choose if and when to upgrade. See our pricing for details.
Is my data private and secure?
Your data is yours. We use it only to run your shipments for you, never to share or sell. Connections use secure, read-scoped access, and you can disconnect at any time.
What happens if the AI gets something wrong?
You're always the final check. logee shows its work, links back to the source email or document, and asks before it acts, so you can correct anything before it matters. It gets more accurate the more you use it.
Do I have to switch carriers, forwarders or my ERP?
No. logee works with how you already ship. Keep your carriers, forwarders, brokers and systems. logee sits on top and pulls everything into one place.
