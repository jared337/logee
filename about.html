About logee

Freight, made effortless
for small teams

The tools that run global freight were built for enterprise. We're building the one for everyone else: the importers and exporters who move real cargo without a big operations team behind them.

Talk to any small importer or exporter and you hear the same thing. The shipment lives across dozens of email threads. Nobody knows the real cost until the invoices are reconciled weeks later. A deadline slips, and the first sign is a demurrage bill. And when the one person who knows the shipments is away, everything grinds to a halt.

Big transport management systems exist, but they're heavy, expensive, and built for enterprises with teams to feed them data. Small teams are left running world-class supply chains out of an inbox and a spreadsheet.

So we built logee: an AI transport management system designed for how small teams actually ship. You set up a shipment, get quotes from your forwarders, and book. From there logee tracks the journey and the conversations, checks every invoice, and warns you before problems turn into costs. It works with the email and tools you already use, and it never acts without your say-so.

Our goal is simple. Give a two-person import business the same visibility and control that a Fortune 500 logistics team takes for granted, without the cost, the complexity, or the months of onboarding.
